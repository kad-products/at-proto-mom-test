---
title: Moving house
order: 1
---

Imagine moving and having to leave all your furniture behind because it's bolted to the old house's floor. That's how most apps work today.

- **Where it fits:** Your stuff is yours. You can leave the "house" without losing it.
- **Where it breaks:** With AT Proto, many apps can use your stuff at the same time. You can't live in two houses at once.
