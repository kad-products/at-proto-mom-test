---
title: Dishes and the dishwasher
order: 4
---

Imagine buying a new dishwasher and being told your old dishes don't fit, so you have to buy all new ones. That's what switching social apps is like today.

- **Where it fits:** Your stuff and the app should be separate. You should be able to replace the appliance and keep your dishes.
- **Where it breaks:** Your dishes don't live in the dishwasher. Your posts do live somewhere, and where they live matters.
