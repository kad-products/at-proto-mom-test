Someone on Gmail can email someone on Outlook. Nobody has to join the same company to talk.

- **Where it fits:** Different apps can work together because they all speak the same language.
- **Where it breaks:** Your emails still live on Google's or Microsoft's computers, and you can't easily pick them up and move them. With AT Proto, your stuff lives in a personal data store you can move to a different host whenever you want.
