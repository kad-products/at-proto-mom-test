## What is the AT Protocol?

Everything you've posted online, like photos, videos, years of posts and the people who follow you, lives inside the app you posted it on. Leave Facebook, and it all stays behind.

The AT Protocol is a different way to build apps. Your posts and your followers belong to **you**, not the app. Different apps can use the same stuff, and you can switch apps without starting over.

Bluesky is the best-known app built this way, but it's just one of many.

## Why "the Mom Test"?

Most explanations of this are written for programmers. We think it matters for everyone, perhaps even more for non-programmers. So my mom and I are working through it together, using plain words, drawings and everyday comparisons. We figure if we can get it to make sense to my mom and her friends, it's ready for everyone.
